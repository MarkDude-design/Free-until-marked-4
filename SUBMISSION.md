## Abstract (~160 words)
A bus. A crash. One question with teeth: who is driving?

The bus one-shot runs crash → who's driving → return path → TRIP. The thesis isn't whether the machine can drive the bus — it's that role-fit beats raw capability, and authority must have a visible return path. What you let drive matters less than whether the thing behind the wheel actually fits the role, and whether the people it carries can see how it gets home.

Twenty minutes, one bus, and the difference between handing over the wheel and handing over the road.
